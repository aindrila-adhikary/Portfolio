About Me

Hey there! I'm someone who gets way too excited about SEO and creating content that actually does something whether that's ranking on Google or making someone pause their scroll for a second longer.

Rather than stepping away entirely, I chose to freelance through the storm taking on work that fit within my capacity while showing up fully for my family when it mattered most. Although the battle was ultimately lost, the experience reshaped my outlook on resilience, purpose, and growth. Today, I am re-entering my career with renewed focus, maturity, and a deep commitment to meaningful, impactful work.

The weight of it never fully lifts, but I have. I'm back with big ideas, fresh energy, and a solid to-do list of everything I missed (starting with chasing the things I'm truly passionate about). Let's just say: this comeback? It's personal.
